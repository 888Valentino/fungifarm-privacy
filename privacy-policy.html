Fungi Farm — Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 1, 2026

Fungi Farm ("the game") is a single-player idle game. This policy explains what happens to your data. Short version: we don't collect any.

Data we collect

None. The game does not collect, store on any server, sell, or share personal information. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics, and no tracking.

Data stored on your device

Your progress (spores, helpers, colony, agent names, settings) is saved only on your own device so you can continue where you left off. It never leaves your device. Deleting the game deletes this data.

Permissions

The game does not use your camera, microphone, contacts, location, or photos. Vibration is used for tap feedback and can be turned off in Settings.

Children

The game is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes

If a future version adds online features (for example a leaderboard), this page will be updated before that version is released, and the change will be described here.
